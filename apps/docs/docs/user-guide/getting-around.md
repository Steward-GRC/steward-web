# Getting around

Every page has the same frame: a header across the top, a menu down the left, and a footer.

## 🧭 The header

- **Steward** at the top left takes you back to **My work**. Your organisation's name sits beside
  it.
- **Search** finds policies, procedures and categories. Click the search box, or press ⌘K (Ctrl+K
  on Windows) from anywhere, to open the search panel; see [Search](/user-guide/search). If you
  write policies, the panel also starts new ones: **New policy**, **New procedure** and **New
  draft from a template**. **Shift+C** opens it straight on these, and **⌘↵** (Ctrl+Enter)
  opens a result in a new tab.
- **Admin** appears if you can manage Steward, and opens the admin app.
- **Feedback** and **Docs** are next, then the bell for your notifications.
- Your initial, at the far right, opens your account menu: **Your profile**, **Notifications**,
  **About and diagnostics** and **Sign out**.

## 🚩 Messages at the top of the page

A strip at the top of the page tells you about something that affects every page:

- **You are acting as …** (with your name) — a site administrator is using Steward as you see it, to help
  you. It shows the minutes left, every action is recorded under the administrator's own
  account, and **Stop** ends it. It can't be hidden while it lasts.
- **Can't reach the server — reconnecting…** — your connection or Steward dropped. It clears by
  itself once Steward answers again.
- **Add an authenticator app or passkey for stronger sign-in** — you only sign in with email
  codes. **Set up** adds a stronger one; the × hides the message for a day.

## 📚 The menu

The menu on the left groups everything you can use:

- **Overview** — My work.
- **Policies** and **Procedures** — the libraries, and the policies waiting for you to
  acknowledge.
- **Assist** — ask a question about your policies, or start from a template if you write them.
- **Workflow** — Approvals (with the number waiting for you) and the audit log, if you approve or
  audit.
- **Browse by category** — open a category to see its subcategories.

You only see what your role lets you use. **Collapse** at the bottom shrinks the menu to icons;
hover an icon to see its name. On a narrower screen the menu is icons only, and on a phone it
opens from the menu button at the top left.

Some entries are still being built. They open a **Coming soon** page until they're ready.

## ♿ Accessibility

The round button at the bottom right of every page, including sign-in, opens **Accessibility
options**:

- **Text size** — Small, Default, Large or X-Large, for every page.
- **Display** — **High contrast** (black and white with a yellow accent), **Reduce motion**
  (stops animations and smooth scrolling; Steward also follows your system's reduced-motion
  setting), **Readable spacing** (more room between letters, words and lines) and **Underline
  links**.
- **Read aloud** — **Read** reads the page's main content aloud with your browser's voice;
  **Pause**, **Resume** and **Stop** control it, and you can change the **Speed** and the
  **Voice**. Some browsers can't read aloud, and the panel says so.

Your choices save to this browser and apply straight away. **Reset** puts them all back. The
light or dark theme is under **Your profile** → **Preferences**.
