# Acknowledge a document

This page shows you how to confirm that you have read a policy or procedure you were asked to
read.

When your organisation needs proof that you have read a document, it asks you to **acknowledge**
it. To acknowledge means to confirm you have read and understood it.

## ✅ How it works

1. **Open the document.** Find it through your dashboard, or through
   [search](/user-guide/search).
2. **Read it.** Read it from start to finish, not just the title.
3. **Confirm.** Select **Acknowledge** and confirm in the dialog. This means "I have read and
   understood this document." Steward records your name and the date.

That is all it takes. Your organisation now has a clear record that you did your part.

## 🧾 Your record

Once you confirm, your organisation has a clear record that you read the document, with your name
and the date. As long as you remain in the audience that document applies to, that record stands
— you will not be asked to sign the same version again.

If you later leave the audience for a document — you are moved out of its group, or it no longer
needs acknowledgement — your record for it is cleared. If you are ever brought back and asked
again, you simply read it and confirm fresh.

Some documents also ask you to acknowledge again when a new version comes out. If that happens,
you will see it in your list once more. Read the new version and confirm again.

:::warning
Read the whole document before you confirm. Acknowledging means you have read and understood it.
:::
