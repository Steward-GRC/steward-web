# Search

This page shows you how to find the policy or procedure you need and open it to read.

## 🔎 Ways to find a document

- **Search** — Click the search box in the header, or press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows), and type a
  word or phrase. The panel shows matching policies, procedures and categories as you type, with
  the matching words underlined. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click, to open one; **See all
  results** opens the full search page. With nothing typed, the panel lists what you opened from
  it recently. This is the fastest way when you know part of the name or a keyword — for
  example, typing `leave` to find the annual-leave policy.
- **Browse the library** — The Policies and Procedures pages open on a tile for each category.
  Pick a category, or one of its subcategories, to list its documents. **Status** in the header
  lists every document (**All policies**) or only those in one state, and **Starred** shows only
  the documents you've starred. Each filter shows in the **Filtered by** bar with the number of
  results; remove one with its ×, or **Clear** them all to go back to the tiles. Select a column
  heading to sort by it, and use the page numbers under the list to move through a long one. If
  you write policies, **New policy** (or **New procedure**) and **Import** are in the header too.
- **The search page** — **See all results** in the search panel opens the search page, which
  lists everything that matches, grouped the same way. Its search box understands `"exact
  phrase"`, `-exclude`, `OR` and `field:value` (for example `category:Finance`).
- **Ask** — **Ask policies & procedures**, in the menu under Assist, answers a question in plain
  language from the policies and procedures you can read. Each part of the answer is numbered,
  and the numbers link to the sections it came from. Always check the source policy before you
  act on an answer. When your organisation's AI is off, Ask says it's temporarily unavailable;
  everything else keeps working.

:::tip
Browse to the right part of the tree first, then search within it — handy when a common word
would otherwise match many documents across the whole library.
:::

## 📄 Reading a document

Open a document to read it on screen. The page shows the document's title, category, version and
current status (see [the lifecycle](/concepts/lifecycle)), along with a sensitivity badge when the
document is marked sensitive.

### History and changes

If a document has been published more than once, its **history** lists every version as a
timeline of submit, review, decision and publish events.

### When something can't be shown

A link to a document that doesn't exist (or that you can't see) shows **Policy not found** with a
way back to the library, and an address that doesn't exist shows **Page not found**. If a page
fails to load, it says **Something went wrong on this page** with an error report: **Copy for
support** copies the details to send to your administrator, and **Retry** loads the page again. A **changes** view highlights what was
added or changed, word by word, so even a small edit is easy to spot.

:::note
You only see documents you are allowed to see. Search and browse both respect your access, so a
document you have no **Read** access to simply will not appear — even in search results. If you
cannot find a document you expected, it may be restricted; ask your administrator, who can check
the category's access rules. See [Who can do what](/concepts/access-model).
:::
