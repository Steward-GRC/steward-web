# User Guide

This section is for everyday staff who use Steward to read, write and act on policies and
procedures.

Here you will find simple, step-by-step help for the things most people do. You do not need a
special role to read documents. If you write drafts, approve them, or handle compliance reports,
this section covers that too.

You reach Steward at your organisation's address and sign in with your username and password.
Staff work at the main site; setup and administration live under `/admin` and are covered in the
[Admin Guide](/admin-guide).

## 🧭 What you will learn here

- **[Signing in and passwords](/user-guide/signing-in)** — how to sign in, reset a password, and
  use one-time codes.
- **[Your home dashboard](/user-guide/your-dashboard)** — what you see when you sign in.
- **[Search](/user-guide/search)** — how to find a policy or procedure.
- **[Acknowledge a document](/user-guide/acknowledge)** — how to confirm you have read one you
  were asked to read.
- **[Author a draft](/user-guide/author-a-draft)** — how to write a policy or procedure, with the
  authoring editor, and send it for approval.
- **[Review and approve](/user-guide/review-and-approve)** — for approvers: review a submitted
  draft and approve or reject it.
- **[Compliance reporting](/user-guide/compliance-reporting)** — for reporting officers: work a
  compliance case from intake to close.

:::tip
New to Steward? Read [Concepts](/concepts) first. It explains the words used here.
:::

:::note
If you forget your password, use the reset link on the sign-in page. Steward emails you a
one-time code; enter it with a new password to get back in.
:::
