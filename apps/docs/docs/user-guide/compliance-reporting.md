# Compliance reporting

This page is for reporting officers — the people who triage and work compliance cases, such as a
possible data incident. It shows how to move a case from intake to close.

Working a case needs the reporting permission, which your administrator grants per person. See
[Who can do what](/concepts/access-model).

## 📥 The case queue

Open **Reporting cases** to see the queue, filtered to **Open** by default, or switch to **All**
or **Mine**. Each case shows its status and the nearest unmet deadline, if it has one; cases with
a looming deadline sort to the top.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| New | Just received, not yet triaged. |
| In review | Being worked. |
| Needs reporter reply | Waiting on more information from whoever reported it. |
| Risk assessment | The risk to the people affected is being assessed. |
| Notification due | A notice to a regulator or an affected person is owed. |
| Closed | Resolved, with an outcome recorded. |

## 🔎 Work a case

Open a case to see what was reported — what happened, when, where, what kind of information was
involved, and whether it is still happening — alongside two places to write:

- **Messages** — a two-way thread the reporter can see. Use it to ask them follow-up questions.
- **Notes** — internal notes visible only to reporting officers, never to the reporter.

From a case you can also:

- **Assign** it to yourself or another reporting officer.
- **Record a risk assessment** — the kinds of information involved, who is likely to have seen it,
  whether it can be contained, and a decision on whether it rises to the level of a reportable
  breach, with your reasoning.
- **Add a notice** — track a notification owed to a regulator or an affected person, and its
  method and status.

## ✅ Close a case

When the case is resolved, close it with an **outcome** and, where relevant, one or more
**corrective actions** — what will change so this does not happen again. A closing message can go
back to the reporter.
