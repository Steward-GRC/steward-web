# Your profile and theme

Open **Your profile** from the account menu in the top right of any page.

## 🪪 Your name

Your first and last name are how you appear across Steward: on approvals, the policies you author
and the audit history. Change them and choose **Save**.

## 🌗 Theme

Pick how Steward looks:

- **Light** — plum on warm paper.
- **Dark** — a dark background with lilac accents.
- **High contrast** — black and white with a yellow accent, for the strongest contrast.
- **Follow my system** — matches your computer's or phone's light or dark setting. This is the
  default.

The change applies straight away, in the staff app and in admin. It's saved to this browser, so
choose it again on another browser or device.
