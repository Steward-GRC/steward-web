# Your profile, security and notifications

Open **Your profile** from the account menu in the top right of any page. It has four tabs:
**Name**, **Security**, **Preferences** and **Notifications**. Each has its own address, so a link
can open the one it means.

## 🪪 Name

Your first and last name are how you appear across Steward: on approvals, the policies you author
and the audit history. Change them and choose **Save**.

## 🔐 Security

**Security methods** lists how you can prove it's you when you sign in:

- **Email code** — the baseline. It's always there and can't be removed.
- **Authenticator app** — select **Set up**, scan the QR code with your authenticator app, then
  enter the 6-digit code it shows. Once it's added, you can **Rename** or **Remove** it.
- **Passkeys** — under **Passwordless sign-in**, select **Register a passkey** and follow your
  browser's prompt. Next time, choose "Sign in with a passkey" on the sign-in page. Each passkey
  can be renamed or removed.

The "stronger sign-in" reminder at the top of the page leads here; it goes away once you've added
an authenticator app or a passkey.

Under **Sessions**, **Sign out everywhere else** signs you out on every other browser and device,
keeping you signed in here.

## 🌗 Preferences

**Theme** picks how Steward looks:

- **Light** — plum on warm paper.
- **Dark** — a dark background with lilac accents.
- **High contrast** — black and white with a yellow accent, for the strongest contrast.
- **Follow my system** — matches your computer's or phone's light or dark setting. This is the
  default.

The theme applies straight away, in the staff app and in admin. It's saved to this browser, so
choose it again on another browser or device.

**Language** sets the display language for your account. **Match my browser** follows your
browser's language. English is the only language today.

## 🔔 Notifications

Choose how often you hear from Steward:

- **How you're reached** — turn email, in-app and push notices on or off. The line below says
  when your daily and weekly digests go out.
- **What you're notified about** — for each kind of notice (Compliance, Security, Account &
  sign-in, Approvals & workflow, Updates & digests), choose **Immediate**, **Daily digest**,
  **Weekly digest** or **Off**. Notices marked **Required** keep a record that's needed for
  audits, so they can't be turned off.
- **Fine-tune** — set single notices on their own. A kind with any notice set this way is marked
  **Custom**.

The **Notifications** link in the account menu, and in Steward's emails, opens this tab.

## 📬 Your inbox

The bell at the top of every page opens your inbox. The in-app inbox isn't available yet; until
it is, approvals and acknowledgements still reach you by email, and **My work** lists what's
waiting on you.
