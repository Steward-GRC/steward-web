# Catalog and policy actions

This page shows you how to find any policy in the organisation and look after it: export it,
share it with a magic link, give it a new author, move it, retire it or delete it.

## 📚 The Catalog

**Catalog** under **Policies** lists every policy across the organisation, in the same table
people use in the staff app: the category tiles and their subcategories, the status filter,
starred policies, and sorting by any column. Open a policy to read it.

## 🛠️ The Actions menu

A policy (or procedure) opened from the admin console reads exactly as it does for staff. Its
**Actions** menu adds a governance group under **Print / Export** and **History & approvals**:

| Action              | What it does                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Export PDF**      | Renders the published version to a PDF, then offers **Download PDF**. A sensitive policy's PDF is watermarked. It needs a published version.                   |
| **Magic link**      | Opens the magic link manager. **Create for v1.0.0** issues a read-only link to the published version, with its QR code, **Copy**, **Revoke** and **Re-issue**. |
| **Change author**   | Hands the policy to someone else: they can then edit its drafts. Site admins only.                                                                             |
| **Move to section** | Moves the policy to another category. It's renumbered to that category's sequence, and the page opens it at its new number. Site admins only.                  |
| **Retire**          | Hides the policy from the catalog and cancels any outstanding acknowledgements. Its version history and the acknowledgements already recorded are kept.        |
| **Delete**          | Permanently removes a policy that was never published, with its draft and appendices. Published policies can't be deleted: retire them instead.                |

:::note
A magic link opens the policy in the staff app's shared reader. A sensitive policy's link asks the
reader for their email first and watermarks what they see. The manager shows the links you make
while the policy is open; links issued earlier aren't listed there yet.
:::
