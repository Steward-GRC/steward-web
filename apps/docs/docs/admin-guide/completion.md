# Completion

**Completion** reports acknowledgement coverage: who has acknowledged a policy, who hasn't,
and whether anyone is overdue. A policy appears here once it is published and has an
acknowledgement trigger set, either on itself or inherited from its owning group (see
[Groups](/admin-guide/groups)).

## 📊 The overview

Open **Completion** under `/admin` to see every policy that requires acknowledgement, with its
acked/pending counts and completion percentage. The **Overdue only** checkbox narrows the list
to policies with at least one overdue user.

## 🔍 One policy's detail

Select a policy's row to drill into its own report: the audience size, how many have
acknowledged, the average days to acknowledge, and how many have viewed the policy without yet
acknowledging it. A **Group** filter scopes the report to one group's audience.

The roster below splits into **Acknowledged** and **Not yet** tabs, each listing the users in
that state. **Export CSV** downloads the full roster.
