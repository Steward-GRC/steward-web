# Completion

**Completion** reports acknowledgement coverage: who has acknowledged a policy, who hasn't,
and whether anyone is overdue. A policy appears here once it is published and has an
acknowledgement trigger set, either on itself or inherited from its category (see
[Groups](/admin-guide/groups)).

## 📊 The overview

**Completion** under **Assurance** lists every policy that requires acknowledgement. Four cards
sum what's shown: **Obligating policies**, **Audience**, **Acknowledged** and **Completion**.
Narrow the list with **Category**, or with **Overdue only** for the policies where someone is
past due. Each row shows the policy, its category, "167 of 214" acknowledged, a progress bar
with the percentage, and a red badge with how many people are overdue.

## 🔍 One policy's detail

Select a policy to open its own report, headed by its title, number and version:

- Five cards: **Audience**, **Acknowledged**, **Completion**, **Avg days to ack** and **Viewed
  not acked** (people who opened the policy but haven't acknowledged it).
- **Acknowledged vs not yet**, a ring with the percentage.
- **Activity over time**, acknowledgements per day for the last **30 days** or **90 days**.
- How many people are overdue.
- The roster's **Acknowledged** and **Not yet** tabs. Overdue people lead **Not yet**, marked
  **Overdue**; **Acknowledged** shows the date each person acknowledged.

**Export CSV** downloads the full roster, for a review meeting.
