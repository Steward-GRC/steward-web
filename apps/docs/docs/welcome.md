# 👋 Welcome to the Steward guide

This guide helps you use Steward. It explains what Steward does and shows you how to use it,
step by step.

Steward is where your organisation keeps its policies and procedures — the written rules and
steps that tell people how to do things — in one place, with a record of who has read what.
Instead of hunting through email or shared drives, you come here to find, read and confirm the
one that matters to you.

This guide is written in plain language. You do not need to be a computer expert to follow it.

## 🧭 The four areas of this guide

This guide is organised into four parts:

- **[Concepts](/concepts)** — Start here if you are new. It explains the big ideas: what a policy
  or procedure is, how one is made, and what it means to acknowledge one.
- **[User Guide](/user-guide)** — For everyday staff. It shows you how to find a document, read
  it, acknowledge it, write a draft, and report a concern.
- **[Admin Guide](/admin-guide)** — For administrators. It shows you how to set up Steward,
  manage people and organisations, and read the audit log.
- **[Reference](/reference)** — A short dictionary of terms you can look up any time.

## 💡 New here?

If this is your first visit, read [Concepts](/concepts) first. It gives you the words and ideas
the rest of the guide uses.

:::note
Ethics reporting — a channel separate from compliance incidents — ships in a later Steward
release. This guide covers what v0.1.0 ships: authoring, approvals, acknowledgement, search,
and compliance reporting.
:::
