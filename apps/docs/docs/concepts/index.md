# Concepts

This section explains the big ideas behind Steward. Read it before you start, and the rest of
the guide will make more sense.

If some words here are new to you, this is the place to begin. These pages explain what a policy
or procedure is, how categories group them, how a document is made and published, and what it
means to acknowledge one.

## 🧭 What you will learn here

- **[What a policy or procedure is](/concepts/what-is-a-policy)** — the two document types, and
  how they are grouped into categories that share settings.
- **[The document lifecycle](/concepts/lifecycle)** — the stages a draft moves through, from a
  first version to a published document.
- **[Acknowledgement](/concepts/acknowledgement)** — what it means to acknowledge a document, who
  has to do it, and when.
- **[Who can do what (access model)](/concepts/access-model)** — how Steward decides who can
  read, acknowledge, approve and author in each category.

:::tip
Read these pages in order. Each one builds on the one before it.
:::
